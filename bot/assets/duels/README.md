## Opening prompt

Create a premium wide landscape 16:9 anime fantasy duel opening background for a Discord bot. Original scene: two opposing explosive streams of silver-white energy clash in the exact center above a shattered stone arena, dramatic dark charcoal storm sky, cinematic depth, delicate sparks and debris, strong manga linework and painterly atmosphere. Monochrome neutral silver and black so software can tint the two halves with player colors. No characters, no faces, no text, no symbols or letters. Leave dark low-detail top strip and bottom strip for titles; leave dark spaces at left center and right center for large avatar portraits. The central energy collision should be brilliant and dynamic, polished full illustration, not a UI mockup.

## Victory prompt

Premium wide landscape 16:9 anime manga finishing-strike background for a Discord fantasy duel victory screen. Original dramatic diagonal silver-white cutting energy slash from lower left to upper right, speed lines radiating across dark charcoal ink background, bright impact burst at right center and splintered geometric shards flying rightwards. Left center dark open area for winner avatar, right center open area for loser avatar to be composited by software. No people, no faces, no words or lettering, no logos. Neutral grayscale silver and black only so effects can be tinted with winner profile palette. High quality hand-painted manga impact with crisp detail, cinematic contrast. Keep top and bottom dark negative space for clear winner and loser name labels. Not a user interface screenshot.
